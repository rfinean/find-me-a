Frequently Asked Questions

Is Find Me A… really free?

Yes, entirely. You pay nothing to search, nothing to view results, and nothing to contact the providers we find. The service is kept running by voluntary tips from happy users. If a lead turns into real work, please consider leaving a small tip — 2% of the contract value is our suggestion, but anything is appreciated.

How does the search work?

We use an AI agent to search the web, professional directories, review sites, and local listings for providers who match your specific request and are geographically close to you. The agent reads reviews, verifies contact details, and ranks results by relevance and proximity.

Why does it take a few minutes?

Unlike a simple keyword search, our AI agent actively browses multiple sources in real time — directories, Google Maps, Trustpilot, Yelp, professional associations, and more. This takes a little longer than a database lookup but produces far richer, more relevant results. We cache results so subsequent searches for the same need in the same area are much faster.

Why do you need my email address?

Because the search can take a minute or two, we email you a direct link to your results so you do not have to stay on the page. We do not share your email with anyone, do not send marketing emails, and do not store it longer than necessary. The only email you will receive is the results link, plus a gentle reminder about tips if you found the lead valuable.

What kinds of things can I search for?

Almost anything where you need a local expert or service: medical specialists, tradespeople, legal advice, tutors, childcare, pet services, financial advisers, therapists, mechanics, caterers — the list is open-ended. The more specific your description, the better the results. For example, “physiotherapist specialising in lower back pain” will produce better results than just “physio”.

How accurate are the results?

We do our best to find genuine, up-to-date information. However, businesses move, close, or change their services, so always verify contact details directly. We display third-party review scores as found; we do not manufacture or alter them. Results are cached for up to six months, after which they are refreshed automatically.

Can I get results without providing a postcode?

A postcode (or zip code / area code) is required to sort results by proximity. Without it we cannot tell you which providers are conveniently close to you. We only use it for geographic sorting and do not store or share your location.

What does “Tell me more…” do on the results page?

Each result has a chat button that opens a conversation powered by an AI assistant. You can ask follow-up questions about that specific provider — what conditions they treat, whether they accept new patients, typical wait times, pricing, and so on. The assistant draws on the same research used to compile the result.

Are you affiliated with any of the providers listed?

No. We have no commercial relationship with any provider. Results are ranked purely by relevance and proximity. Nobody pays to appear, and nobody can pay to be removed. Our only revenue is voluntary user tips.

How do I leave a tip?

Use the Ko-fi or PayPal buttons at the top of this page. Ko-fi lets you contribute as little as £3 (“one coffee”) in a couple of clicks. PayPal accepts any amount. Both are secure and you do not need an account to pay via PayPal. We suggest roughly 2% of the value of any contract or work you commission as a result of a lead, but any amount is genuinely appreciated.

My results look out of date. What can I do?

Run the search again — if the cached data is more than six months old it will be automatically refreshed. If you spot a specific error (wrong phone number, closed business, etc.) please email us at hello@findmea.example and we will update the record.

How is my data protected?

Your email is used only to send you the results link. Your search terms and postcode are stored to build the results cache, improving speed for future users in the same area, but they are not linked to your email. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. Cached search data is deleted after six months.
